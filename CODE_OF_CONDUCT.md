# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TextLens contributors, maintainers, and community participants are expected to create a professional, welcoming environment.

Positive behavior includes respectful technical feedback, crediting contributions, focusing disagreement on evidence and user impact, and respecting privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or community content that violates this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in participation restrictions.

For conduct concerns, contact `sanskarin@outlook.in`.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person is publicly representing the TextLens project.
